Services

Hengxin Professional Services offers accounting and audit, tax advisory and company secretarial services to businesses in Kuala Lumpur.

Services

Accounting & Audit

We provide day-to-day bookkeeping, monthly and annual financial statements, and support during audits for small and medium businesses. Clear, accurate accounts help you understand how the business is doing and lay the groundwork for financing and compliance.

Tax Advisory

We offer tax planning, filing preparation and compliance advice. We help you understand your tax obligations, file on time, and consider the tax side of everyday business decisions.

Company Secretarial

We assist with company incorporation, statutory records and registers, changes of directors and shareholders, and annual filings, so your company stays compliant.
