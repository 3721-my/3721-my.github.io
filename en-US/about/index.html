About Us

Hengxin Professional Services is a professional services firm in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur. We offer three services to local businesses: accounting and audit, tax advisory, and company secretarial.

Our philosophy

"Hengxin" stands for lasting, reliable trust. We believe financial and compliance support should be clear, accurate and delivered on time, so business owners can focus on their business.

How we work

  • Clear communication: we explain numbers and regulatory requirements in plain language.
  • On-time delivery: we remind you ahead of filing and tax deadlines.
  • One team: accounting, tax and company secretarial matters are coordinated by the same team, so documents don't pass back and forth.
